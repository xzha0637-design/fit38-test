# Known limitations

- Twitter/X public-data scope only.
- Offline fixtures only; no live platform access.
- The demonstration requires locally trained artifacts.
- Full graph and content-semantic features are excluded.
- Model performance can shift across datasets, time periods, and prevalence.
- The Stage 00 browser shell does not yet expose account intake controls.
- The model is a prioritisation aid and cannot establish whether an account is
  automated, harmful, fraudulent, or policy-violating.
